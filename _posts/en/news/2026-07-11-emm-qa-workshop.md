On July 11, 2026, Ikuya Yamada co-organized [EMM-QA](https://qanta-org.github.io/competition/2026/icml/), a workshop on efficient multimodal question answering, at ICML 2026 in Seoul, South Korea.

The program included invited talks, paper presentations, highlights from the [QANTA 2026](https://qanta-org.github.io/competition/2026/) shared task, and a quiz event in which humans and AI competed together as teams.
