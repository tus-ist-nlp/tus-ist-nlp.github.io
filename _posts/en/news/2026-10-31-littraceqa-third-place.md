Team “tus-nlp,” including Akira Ise (a fourth-year undergraduate student in our lab), placed third in the LitTraceQA competition held at [GroundLM 2026](https://groundlm.github.io/grouplm_emnlp2026/), a workshop co-located with EMNLP 2026.

LitTraceQA challenges AI systems to retrieve scientific papers, locate supporting evidence, and answer questions accurately.

Our team developed Lit3R, a system that retrieves papers relevant to a question and expands its search using relationships between papers. It then combines evidence from multiple papers to generate an answer.

**Competition report:**<br>
[Findings of the First GroundLM Shared Tasks: Evaluating Grounded Language Models Across Visual and Scientific Evidence](https://aclanthology.org/2026.groundlm-1.92/)

**System paper:**<br>
[Lit3R: Retrieve–Relate–Read for Evidence-Grounded Question Answering over Scientific Literature](https://aclanthology.org/2026.groundlm-1.89/)
