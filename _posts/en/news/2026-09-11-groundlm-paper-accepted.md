Our paper “Lit3R” has been accepted to [GroundLM 2026](https://groundlm.github.io/grouplm_emnlp2026/), a workshop at EMNLP 2026. The first author is Akira Ise, a fourth-year undergraduate student in our lab.

**Title:** Lit3R: Retrieve–Relate–Read for Evidence-Grounded Question Answering over Scientific Literature<br>
**Authors:** Akira Ise, Kotaro Kumagai, Yuta Yamaguchi, Hisanori Ozaki, Yukio Uematsu, Ikuya Yamada
